The new Frutiger Aero themed launcher for Android is here!


Bring back the future!

Turn your phone into the retro-futuristic dream of the 2000s with Frutiger Launcher — a launcher inspired by the iconic glossy, optimistic design era of classic operating systems like Windows 7.

Rediscover the look of a future we once imagined: glassy buttons, vivid gradients, soft reflections, nature-inspired interfaces, and that unmistakable sleek aesthetic of the past.

With Frutiger Launcher, your device becomes more than a phone — it becomes a nostalgic vision of tomorrow — the promised future we never got.

Using Frutiger Launcher you can apply different themes and customize your device to your liking. The currently available themes are:

Frutiger Aero

Frutiger Eco

Helvetica Aqua Aero

Dorfic

Dark Aero

Frutiger Metro

If you would like to customize your UI/background any further, feel free to choose one of the 6 styles the app offers you! (more coming soon)
